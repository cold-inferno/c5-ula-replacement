Configuration mode

Settings can only be read and written by the bootloader, which listens for 2 seconds after the C5 is switched on.

  1. Connect the USB-serial adapter and click Connect USB, or click Connect Bluetooth and pick your AT-09.
  2. Click Enter configuration mode.
  3. Switch the C5 off and on again within 20 seconds. (Over Bluetooth the link drops while the module is unpowered; the tool reconnects by itself.)

Ride log

Needs configuration mode (see Settings) and firmware V1202+ with the ride log switched on. Reading takes about 10 seconds over USB and longer over Bluetooth.

Upload

Needs configuration mode (see Settings). Your settings are kept. If the upload is interrupted, the bootloader stays intact: just enter configuration mode and upload again.

Firmware upload is switched off over Bluetooth: a dropped link would leave half a firmware on the chip. Connect the USB-serial adapter for this.
