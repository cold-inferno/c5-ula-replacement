Configuration mode

Settings can only be read and written by the bootloader, which listens for 2 seconds after the C5 is switched on.

  1. Connect the USB-serial adapter and click Connect.
  2. Click Enter configuration mode.
  3. Switch the C5 off and on again within 20 seconds.
